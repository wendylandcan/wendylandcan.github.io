About me

From law to AI.

My first reason to build a product was simple: help my colleagues spend less time comparing orders. After building my first review workflow with Dify at Lilith, I brought the same curiosity to enterprise legal work, labor-law consultations, and even a friend’s “couples courtroom.”

AI Product Intern

NIO Legal AI Platform

A single entry point for legal questions, knowledge, and case collaboration.

Project details · NIO Legal AI Platform
Problem
Employees reached legal through scattered channels. Repeat questions were hard to capture, and case teams lacked a shared view.
My role
I helped build the platform from zero to one, designing modules for intake, cases, law firms, knowledge, and updates. I designed Feishu Q&A, two knowledge retrieval paths, and escalation to legal staff, then evaluated citations and follow-up questions through replay and legal review.

Legal AI

Product Lead

JobPilot

A labor-law agent for workers, employers, and lawyers that asks for missing facts before providing analysis with sources.

Project details · JobPilot
Problem
Labor-law questions often leave out contract details, dates, and jurisdiction. Any of those facts can change the analysis.
My role
I mapped 24 scenarios and led a three-stage agent design for scenario recognition, fact-finding, and analysis. Each user group gets tailored questions and reports. The system retrieves laws and cases through MCP and uses rules-based calculators for compensation and other figures.
Outcome
The team placed second among 173 entries in the law firm track of the second iCourt National Legal AI Competition.

Independent Build

Design & Development

Liqingai

A two-person interactive courtroom that gives both partners space to tell their side.

Project details · Liqingai
Problem
A generic AI chat often follows the account it hears first. In a disagreement, the other person may never get to provide context or say how they feel.
My role
I built a five-step interaction covering statements, evidence, challenges, debate, and an AI verdict. It supports screenshot and audio analysis, live synchronization, and streamed verdicts.
Outcome
Routing tasks across models and caching results reduced API request costs for complex cases by more than 60%.
60%+Lower API cost per complex case

Concept & prototype

Solo, Together

Some days on a solo trip, I’d love to share a meal or a walk. I’m exploring whether AI can help solo travelers find people whose pace and plans are a good fit.

Explore the concept

The prototype offers two entry points: finding a companion for part of an itinerary, or finding someone for a meal nearby. AI turns a plain-language request into a plan and explains where budgets, tastes, and travel pace line up or differ. Both people agree on the details before joining. User validation is still ahead.
